## 🥊 Competitor watch

**1. Shiftly Auto (10,000+ users, updated July 17, 2026)**
Shiftly is the market leader by user count. Dealers know the name. Their Trustpilot reviews are real and positive. But Shiftly is Facebook Marketplace only, no multi-platform reach, no AI descriptions in dealer voice, no manager dashboard showing who posted what.
*Ventrix counter:* Don't attack Shiftly by name. Just show what Shiftly can't do. Manager dashboard with posted-by-name tracking, AI descriptions, 5/30/60 min lead pings. Let dealers do the math.

**2. CARVID (Facebook enforcement crackdown, May 2026)**
CARVID is smart. They saw Facebook's stricter enforcement (30-day bans on first violation, permanent on second within 90 days) and turned it into a sales tool. They're claiming zero bans across 73 dealers. The fear angle is real because the fear is real.
*Ventrix counter:* Acknowledge the fear, then neutralize it fast and move to speed. Ventrix posts from real salesperson profiles, native Facebook compliance, smart spacing built in. Then pivot to the 22 min vs 10-15 sec advantage. Ban safety is a tie-breaker, not the headline.

**3. AutoLister Pro (429+ dealers, July 12, 2026)**
AutoLister undercuts Shiftly on price and posts to multiple platforms. They're winning on the "cheaper and flexible" angle. But their pitch is about price, not speed.
*Ventrix counter:* Speed beats price every time in a dealership. If a salesperson can post 12 cars in the time it used to take to post 1, that's not a software cost — that's a commission opportunity. Make that math visible in every salesperson-targeted post.
