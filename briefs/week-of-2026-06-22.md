**1. CARVID — Compliance Fear Offensive**
CARVID is running hard on Facebook's new 30-day ban policy, positioning "ban protection" as a premium feature inside an expensive platform. Their angle: pay us or lose your account.
*Ventrix counter:* Ventrix is browser-based, one post at a time, no mass-posting bots. That's structurally the safest posting method on the market — and it costs a fraction of what CARVID charges. Post the message simply: "No bots. No bulk triggers. One listing at a time. Zero ban risk by design."

Shiftly is generating 50K+ TikTok views by selling waived onboarding fees and influencer energy to salespeople. But their reviews are 1-star, their pricing is hidden, and they're not converting dealership decision-makers — only reps who can't actually buy the software.
*Ventrix counter:* Go straight at the decision-makers Shiftly ignores. Manager dashboard, posted-by-name accountability, and founder-handled concierge setup days 1–5 are things a Used Car Manager or Dealer Principal actually cares about. While Shiftly sells hype, Ventrix sells proof.

Marketplace Pro launched in Feb 2026 with direct AutoTrader/Cars.com/CarGurus syncs. Sounds powerful until you realize most independent dealers and individual reps don't have clean DMS integrations — and nobody wants a 2-week onboarding before they can post a single car.
*Ventrix counter:* Zero integrations required. Open Chrome, open the listing, hit go. 10–15 seconds. Works for the single-location used car lot as well as the 8-rooftop group. Position Ventrix as "the one that works on Day 1."
