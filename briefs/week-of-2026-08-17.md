## 🥊 Competitor watch

**CARVID — 9-platform complexity pitch**
CARVID is selling dealers on managing Facebook, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, Pinterest, X, Bluesky, and Google Business from one dashboard. They're calling it table-stakes. The counter: nine platforms means nine things to learn, nine things to break, and nine reasons your listing didn't go up when your buyer was shopping. Ventrix does one thing in 10-15 seconds on the platform that closes the most car deals. That's the angle.

**Shiftly Auto — onboarding-fee waiving + trust issues**
Shiftly is waiving onboarding fees on TikTok to drive signups, but Whop reviews surface complaints about delivery and feature gaps. Their model requires a "success team call" before you can do anything. Ventrix's counter: install the extension, click Post, it fills the form. No success team. No onboarding call. Works today.

**Owini — "human-like typing simulation" as compliance pitch**
Owini is selling anti-ban protection by making the tool pretend to type like a human. That's slower by definition and ethically fuzzy. Ventrix's counter: we follow Facebook's actual rules (10 posts per day, 5-minute spacing, sold-car auto-removal). You don't need to fake anything when you're posting like a real dealer should.
