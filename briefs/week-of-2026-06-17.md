## 🥊 Competitor watch

**1. CARVID — Feature bloat as a strategy**
CARVID added a TikTok-style video editor, newsletter distribution, blog posting, and one-click social sharing on top of their existing multi-platform tool — and now publicly recommends dealers use CARVID *and* Glo3D together. Their App Store response literally says "adding more and more cool features every day." The counter-angle: **Ventrix does one thing in 12 seconds. Every feature CARVID adds is another reason your team slows down.** Never lead with ban safety against CARVID unless the post is directly comparative.

**2. CARVID — Three-tier pricing confusion**
Standard at $249, Pro at $499, Enterprise at $799 — plus the implicit pressure to upgrade. This is the classic SaaS tier trap. Counter-angle: **Ventrix pricing is transparent and flat. No "call us for enterprise." No feature-gating the tools your floor team actually needs.** Don't name the price point in posts, but the "no tier games" language lands hard.

**3. Shiftly Auto — Hidden pricing + 12-hour usage cap**
Listed at $129/month on Whop, actual negotiated price reportedly ~$1,000/month. Reviews confirm a 12-hour daily usage limitation and slow sales team responsiveness. Counter-angle: **Ventrix is a Chrome extension — always on, no usage cap, no surprises on the invoice.** This is most powerful as a salesperson-facing post because individual reps feel the cap pain personally.
